Our Latest Techniques in LASIK

🗓 15 September 2025

Our latest techniques in LASIK 2025: more advancements are making LASIK safer, more precise, more accessible and more widely applied to eye tissues.

Refractive errors: Myopia, Hyperopia, Presbyopia, Keratoconus and Astigmatism will all be corrected precisely. FemtoSecond technology is now playing a very important role in cataract surgery.

All of the above will be treated precisely through these techniques:

  1. AI-Powered Corneal Mapping
  2. Wavefront-Guided LASIK
  3. Personalized Treatment
  4. Femtosecond Lasers
  5. Presbyopia-Correcting LASIK
  6. SMILE Pro
  7. Topography-Guided LASIK
  8. Adaptive Optics
  9. Telemedicine Integration

Regarding the cornea, we only target 2 of its 5 layers: the Epithelium and the Stroma.

From now on, no setbacks compared with traditional LASIK.
